# FiHay FRMS v2.0 — Hosted Farmer Pilot Test Guide

## Recommended pilot size

Start with FiHay plus 2–4 other farmers. Each farmer registers their own farm workspace on the same hosted URL.

## Test these workflows

1. Register and sign in from a phone.
2. Confirm one farmer cannot see another farmer's plots, crops, sales or livestock.
3. Add a plot and crop cycle; verify planting date + duration calculates expected harvest date.
4. Add weekly crop updates, fertiliser and pesticide applications.
5. Record an expense linked to the crop and attach a receipt while online.
6. Record a harvest and verify marketable produce stock.
7. Sell part of the produce and verify stock reduces; attempt an oversale and verify it is blocked.
8. Create a livestock group with male/female composition; record mating/pregnancy, birth, vaccination/sickness/death.
9. Sell livestock and verify herd numbers reduce automatically.
10. Add farm inventory, e.g. 4 spades; issue one and verify the available quantity changes.
11. Record an incident and a resolution/lesson learned; search it from AI Help.
12. Add a Field Officer account and a Viewer account; confirm the Viewer cannot edit records.
13. On a phone, visit the key screens while online, then disconnect mobile data/Wi-Fi from the internet and test cached screens/queued text entries.
14. Reconnect and verify queued records sync only into the same farm account.
15. Download a farm backup.

## Feedback to collect

- What was easy or confusing?
- Which fields were missing or unnecessary?
- Did terminology make sense to the farmer?
- Was it comfortable on a phone?
- Which activities did they still record on paper or WhatsApp?
- Did they trust crop-stock and livestock-count calculations?
- Did offline queueing behave as expected?
- What reports do they need weekly/monthly?
- What information are they willing to share with BAM in aggregate?

## Do not expand beyond the pilot until

- tenant isolation has been independently reviewed;
- backup and restore have been tested;
- a data ownership/privacy agreement exists;
- farmer consent for aggregated BAM reporting is defined;
- an audit log and account recovery process are added;
- the pilot survives a representative production period.
